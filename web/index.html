INSIDE A SESSION

Everything the party already does, minus the cash.

◉

The wall

A live display for the venue screen: a leaderboard of who has sprayed the most, a feed of sprays as they land, and the running total for the night.

✺

The MC agent

Every owambe has a hype man. Ours watches the sprays on chain and writes live Pidgin commentary onto the wall, calling names and announcing when someone takes the lead.

◫

Automatic splits

The host sets the rules when the session opens: a share to the celebrant, a share to the DJ, a share to the caterer. Nobody settles up at 2am.

✉

Gift, privately

The digital envelope. Same wallet, same payment, but it stays off the public leaderboard.

✈

Spray from abroad

Family who could not travel spray from their phone and get called out by name on the wall, in real time, as if they were in the room.

⚡

Why BOT Chain

Spraying is many tiny payments in quick succession. It only feels right where confirmation is instant and fees are near zero. On a slower chain, one spray would cost more than the spray.
